The question

How can play-by-play information become a practical scouting report for opposing teams?

What I built and explored

  • Prepared NFL play-by-play information for offensive and defensive team profiles.
  • Created situational filters for down, red zone and other game contexts.
  • Built matchup and fourth-down decision analysis views.
  • Generated downloadable HTML scouting reports with Quarto.

Tools and methods

R, Shiny, nflverse, gt, Quarto

Takeaway

The goal was to make detailed sports information more accessible and useful through interactive tools and clear reporting.
