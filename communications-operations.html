01 / THE BIGGER PICTURE

The work that helps game day run.

My Penn State Athletics internships complemented my statistics background with hands-on communications and event management experience. The common thread was delivering accurate information and dependable support in time-sensitive environments.

Behind every event are two essential workflows.

Communications staff prepare statistics, research, and media materials so coaches, teams, broadcasters, and journalists can rely on organized information. Event staff coordinate venues, staff, and fan services to help competition days run smoothly.

I supported both functions as an intern. The responsibilities below describe my own contributions; they do not imply that I independently authored an entire department's publications or directed event operations.
