MLB All-Star Selection Study

A four-person Penn State research project examining how WAR and other batting statistics relate to MLB All-Star selection.

View GitHub ↗

The question

How do WAR and other 2023 MLB batting metrics relate to All-Star selection?

What I built and explored

  • Analyzed a sample of 2023 MLB batting statistics.
  • Investigated WAR and other performance factors in All-Star selection.
  • Explored statistical and machine-learning approaches.
  • Collaborated on an R Markdown report with Joe Redmond, Leo Soccio, and Tyler Sweeney.

Tools and methods

R, R Markdown, statistical analysis, machine learning

Takeaway

This collaborative study illustrates how statistical tools can help evaluate questions about MLB player performance and recognition.
